{# SECTION 1: GETTING IN (Visual 4-Step Onboarding Journey) #}

Onboarding

Getting in

Public pages (Services, Programs, Gallery, and About) are open to everyone. Drafting, reviewing, or managing extension records requires a verified account.

  1. 01

    Register an account

    Open Create Account and enter your name, institutional email, and password. New accounts start as Faculty so proponents can draft right away.

    Default role: Faculty
  2. 02

    Verify your email

    Click the one-time verification link sent to your inbox before signing in. Links expire for security; if yours expires, request a fresh one from the sign-in screen.

    Required before first login
  3. 03

    Open your workspace

    Sign in to land automatically on the dashboard matching your role (Faculty, Coordinator, Evaluator, Staff, Director, or Admin).

    Role-routed dashboard
  4. 04

    Start or review work

    Launch a new proposal from your dashboard or Services, join a colleague's draft as co-proponent, or open assigned reviews.

    Autosaved progress

When can proponents edit wizard steps?

Only in Drafting and For Revision. Co-proponents can update fields, upload revised workbooks, and resubmit when ready.

Why do fields lock after submission?

During Submitted for Review, In Review, and post-approval stages, wizard answers freeze so every reviewer inspects the exact same snapshot.

{# SECTION 3: INSIDE THE WIZARD, COLLABORATORS & REVIEW ROUNDS #}

Authoring & Collaboration

Working inside the Proposal Wizard

The Proposal Wizard adapts its fields and required uploads to your initiative's scope, document format, and extension classification.

Scope & Document Format

On Step 1, choose whether your initiative is a multi-project Program (which unlocks component project titles, phases, and assigned Project Leaders), a standalone Project, or an Activity only, and pick between Extension Proposal and Training Design formatting.

Co-Proponents & Live Presence

Add registered colleagues on Step 3 (Proponents). Co-proponents see the proposal on their own dashboard, can edit unlocked steps alongside you, and see live presence pills indicating which step a teammate is currently viewing. Draft text autosaves as you work.

Section Comments & Rounds

During review, coordinators, evaluators, and the Director attach feedback to individual steps. When a Comment Summary is issued and status moves to For Revision, address the flagged steps and resubmit — earlier rounds remain preserved for comparison.

{# Four Document Lifecycle Cards #}

Official Excel Templates (.xlsx)

Work Plan, Gantt Chart & Line-Item Budget

Download the official .xlsx workbooks directly inside Steps 17 and 18 or from the Template Library on Services. Fill in the sheets without altering column headers and upload the saved workbook back into the wizard.

Signed & Released Documents

Signed Proposal, Award & Endorsement

Once your proposal reaches Ready to Print, print the formatted document, gather wet signatures, and upload the scanned PDF. Extension Staff then release the Letter of Award, Endorsement for Approval, and Extension Agreement to your record.

Guided MOA Builder (.docx)

Automated Memorandum of Agreement

When a partner MOA is required, complete the 4-step MOA form (parties, purpose, mutual obligations, and signatories) to generate a formatted Word document ready for legal review, revision, and final upload.

Terminal Reports & Activity Photos

Field Documentation & Public Gallery Sync

Upload four captioned activity photographs (JPEG, PNG, or WebP, up to 10 MB each) alongside your progress/terminal report. Approved proposals list automatically in Programs and captioned photos publish to the Gallery.

{# SECTION 6: QUARTERLY ACCOMPLISHMENT REPORTS & TARGETS #}

Institutional Monitoring

Quarterly accomplishment reports & annual targets

Beyond individual proposal trackers, NExUS coordinates quarterly accomplishment reporting across departments and campuses.

Step 01 · Request Window

Office opens a quarter

Extension Staff issue a quarterly report request (Q1–Q4) specifying the target year, due date, and instructions for coordinators.

Step 02 · Unit Filing

Coordinators submit figures

Department and Campus Coordinators record accomplished indicators, beneficiary counts, and supporting files directly from their dashboards.

Step 03 · Target Rollup

Campus KPI comparison

Submitted accomplishments aggregate automatically against annual institutional targets so Staff and the Director can monitor progress per campus.

Your account & session protection

{# Password & Login Protection Card #}

Password rules & recovery

  • 8+ characters
  • 1+ uppercase
  • 1+ number
  • Live validation: Password forms check each requirement as you type, warn when Caps Lock is active, and include a visibility toggle.
  • Forgot your password? Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen to receive a single-use reset link.
  • Campus, department & role updates: Contact an Administrator via the Extension Office if you transfer units or take on a coordinator, evaluator, or staff assignment.

Support & Recovery

If something is wrong

Verification email missing

Check your spam or junk folder first, then sign in with your credentials to trigger a fresh verification email.

Account temporarily locked

Repeated failed sign-in attempts trigger a temporary brute-force cooldown. Wait for the window to clear or reset your password via Forgot password?.

Workbook or file rejected

Verify the file matches one of the accepted formats and size limits shown below the upload field, and use the unmodified official .xlsx template on Steps 17–18.
