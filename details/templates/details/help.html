Getting in

  1. 01

    Register

    Sign up with your institutional email, campus, college, and department. New accounts start as Faculty.

  2. 02

    Verify email

    Open the verification link sent to your inbox. If it expires, you can request a fresh link on the sign-in page.

  3. 03

    Sign in

    Sign in with your username or email to open the dashboard for your role and start or review proposals.

Stepped away? Idle sessions end after 10 minutes with a 60-second warning banner so shared computers stay protected.

Account & roles

  • Passwords require at least eight characters, one number, and one uppercase letter. Use Forgot password? on the sign-in page if you need a reset link.
  • Failed sign-in attempts pause login temporarily to protect your account; wait for the cooldown or reset your password.
  • Campus, department, and role changes are managed by an administrator through the Extension Office.
