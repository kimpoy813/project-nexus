It is now a dispatcher: each section names its own template via ``section.block_template``, which comes from the content source registered in ``details.content_sources``. Adding a no-code builder to the page palette therefore means adding one entry to the registry and one template in ``details/blocks/`` — nothing here or in the four public page templates changes.
